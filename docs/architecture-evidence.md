# Evidence record

Keep generated evidence under a new ignored artifact directory, with:

- Source revision, dirty patch, toolchain/build flags, hardware and binary hashes.
- Exact commands, workload inventory, raw samples and named failures.
- Observation scope, phase, units, populations and missing/truncated data.
- The tested hypothesis, controls, competing explanations and conclusion.
- Per-fixture results and complete aggregates only when every fixture is valid.
- Native artifact/entry/exit witnesses for claims about generated execution.

The instruction ledger supplies counter observations, not CPU percentages or
proof of performance. Do not copy top rows into a growing documentation diary.
The report is the record; task closure cites its identity and verification.
Follow [the performance protocol](performance-lanes.md) and
[task completion rules](../tasks/README.md).
