## Code cursor candidate (2026-10-09)

The loop now keeps the active program/function identity and packed/wide code
table pointers in `GeneralCodeCursor`, refreshing it when inline frames return,
are pushed, tail-call replacement occurs, or exception unwinding restores the
caller. The central `step` match, per-instruction PC publication, and
`StepResult` representation are unchanged. The cursor's raw pointers are
limited to one interpreter invocation; the entry residual is borrowed for that
invocation and any switched residual is owned by `current_program`.

On 11 alternating production pairs on Apple M4, retired instructions fell
11.86% (bootstrap 95% interval 11.84–11.86%) on a 10M local-store loop and
5.31% (95% interval 5.31–5.32%) on a CJS-shaped 10M call loop; every pair
favored the candidate. Median RSS was unchanged. `__TEXT` grew by 16 KiB,
including 7,616 bytes in `__text`. The runtime library suite passed 530/530;
focused Test262 stages passed except the already-confirmed baseline
foreign-realm `Function.call` mismatch (508/509 in stage 48), and the Wasm
suite passed 67,124/67,124. Raw pairs, source/binary hashes, host details and
test counts are in the [code-cursor evidence](evidence/task61-dispatch-code-cursor-2026-10-09.json).
