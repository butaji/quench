- Reports identify source, binaries, toolchain, commands, suite revisions,
  inventories, and host; they become task 27's cutover baseline.

Support tasks own any required fixes, but completing their historical plans is
not an independent prerequisite. This gate checks the three suite results and
necessary internal invariants directly. No performance ranking, fuzzing
campaign, host expansion, or kernel rewrite blocks it.
