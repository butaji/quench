# 61: Lead no-JIT engines on every V8-v7 benchmark

## Outcome

After Stage A is verified, on the execution host recorded in the evidence
(Linux x86_64 KVM in this workspace), Quench achieves the highest median Score
and lowest median maximum RSS on each V8-v7 benchmark: Crypto,
DeltaBlue, EarleyBoyer, NavierStokes, RayTrace, RegExp, Richards, and Splay.
Compare against QuickJS, Bun/JSC with JIT disabled, and Node/V8 --jitless.
Both targets must hold on every benchmark, not just the aggregate.

1. Run all four engines on each benchmark and keep one immutable
   fixture/round row with the four matched Score and maximum-RSS samples plus
   their versions, commands, effective JIT evidence and binary/input hashes.
   Derive medians and paired intervals from those rows; reject a row if any
   engine sample or RSS value is missing or invalid.
2. Rank the measured gaps. Select only mechanisms needed to close them;
   candidate task specifications guide experiments rather than require them.
   Use measured v2 successes where applicable and cite its negative evidence
   before retrying rejected mechanisms. Follow Ershov binding-time analysis
   and Futamura specialization through shared semantics, resolving proven
   static facts before execution. Use the Self VM research references in
   [README.md](README.md#stage-b--performance) for maps, guarded site-local caches
   and feedback-guided candidate selection; account for metadata and variant
   growth in paired RSS measurements. Consult pinned v2 measurements only when
   they help explain a measured gap, and record the decision in this task's
   evidence rather than opening a separate diagnostic campaign.
3. Admit general changes by paired Score/RSS evidence, with proven static
   assumptions or guards and exact fallback. Remove rejected experiments.
   Preserve supported dynamic compilation and host behavior.
4. After every retained change, run affected Test262 stages plus compare-runs,
   affected Node/Wasm checks, and relevant safety controls. Full suite runs
   belong at stage qualification, not every experiment.
