# 61: Lead no-JIT engines on every V8-v7 benchmark

## Outcome

After Stage A is verified, on Apple M4/macOS Quench achieves the highest
median Score and lowest median maximum RSS on each V8-v7 benchmark: Crypto,
DeltaBlue, EarleyBoyer, NavierStokes, RayTrace, RegExp, Richards, and Splay.
Compare against QuickJS, Bun/JSC with JIT disabled, and Node/V8 --jitless.
Both targets must hold on every benchmark, not just the aggregate.

## Current gap

Correctness and a single VM do not establish performance leadership. Pre-gate
diagnostics may identify likely bottlenecks on the current candidate; qualify
the final Stage A production core and close each measured gap only after task
86.

The pre-gate RegExp matcher pilot in
[task61-regexp-lazy-pre-gate-2026-10-07.json](evidence/task61-regexp-lazy-pre-gate-2026-10-07.json)
improved the three-round median RegExp Score from 20.7 to 32.4, with median
maximum RSS moving from 64,749,568 to 64,077,824 bytes. A one-round screen also
covered all eight fixtures: seven Quench scores rose, while Navier-Stokes fell
2.7%; RSS was lower in all eight samples. These are dirty-tree diagnostics,
not paired qualification; the candidate's highest RegExp RSS sample increased,
and the full eleven-round, eight-benchmark gate is still required.

## Scope and implementation sequence

1. Run all four engines on each benchmark and keep one immutable
   fixture/round row with the four matched Score and maximum-RSS samples plus
   their versions, commands, effective JIT evidence and binary/input hashes.
   Derive medians and paired intervals from those rows; reject a row if any
   engine sample or RSS value is missing or invalid.
2. Rank the measured gaps. Select only mechanisms needed to close them;
   candidate task specifications guide experiments rather than require them.
   Use measured v2 successes where applicable and cite its negative evidence
   before retrying rejected mechanisms. Follow Ershov binding-time analysis
   and Futamura specialization through shared semantics, resolving proven
   static facts before execution. Use the Self VM research references in
   [README.md](README.md#stage-b--performance) for maps, guarded site-local caches
   and feedback-guided candidate selection; account for metadata and variant
   growth in paired RSS measurements. Consult pinned v2 measurements only when
   they help explain a measured gap, and record the decision in this task's
   evidence rather than opening a separate diagnostic campaign.
3. Admit general changes by paired Score/RSS evidence, with proven static
   assumptions or guards and exact fallback. Remove rejected experiments.
   Preserve supported dynamic compilation and host behavior.
4. After every retained change, run affected Test262 stages plus compare-runs,
   affected Node/Wasm checks, and relevant safety controls. Full suite runs
   belong at stage qualification, not every experiment.

## Verification and definition of done

- Use at least 11 rotating paired samples per engine comparison, matched
  production profiles/inputs, and 95% paired intervals for both Score and
  maximum RSS. Reject invalid, missing, mismatched, or drifted samples; do not
  substitute unpaired intervals or Score-only validity for the paired record.
- For every benchmark and every comparator, Quench's median Score is higher
  and median maximum RSS lower, with paired intervals excluding a tie.
- Record ordered raw samples, binaries, source revisions, toolchains, host,
  commands, versions, and proof that reference JIT tiers are disabled.
- Every Node-adjudicated pinned Test262 case passes, every Wasm
  directive passes, and the Stage A Node gate passes for its frozen
  framework-core fixtures and pinned Express, Koa and Fastify scenarios on the
  measured source/configuration. Any exact fixture contradicted by local Node
  and the current ECMAScript algorithm remains an explicit raw-failure waiver,
  never a pass. The broad Node
  inventory is diagnostic, not an additional Stage A gate.
- No benchmark-dependent production behavior, second VM, guest JIT, stencil,
  or executable-memory path is introduced.
