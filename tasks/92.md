# 92: Run parallel lanes in sync with the v2 trunk

## Outcome

Several agents work at once without duplicating mechanisms or drifting apart.
Every retained change reaches `origin/v2` through one integration gate, and
every lane starts its next change from the current trunk.

V8-v7 work follows Task 61's lens: one fixture at a time, kept active until it
clears both the Score bar and the maximum-RSS bar. The active fixture is the
one Task 61 names. A lane that finds its next step inside another lane's area
records the finding in that lane's task instead of implementing it.

## Starting a session

Lanes have no direct communication with each other or with the trunk owner;
`tasks/` on `origin/v2` is the only channel. At the start of every session:

1. Merge `origin/v2` into your branch.
2. Find your lane in the table above by your branch name, and read your
   lane's task file, Task 61 and this file.
3. Read your task's `## Inbox` and act on its open items.

## Communication

- Each lane task file ends with two sections. `## Status` belongs to the
  lane: after each retained commit, the lane replaces it with its current
  step, last result and next step. `## Inbox` receives messages: anyone may
  append a dated item there, and the lane marks an item done when it handles
  it.
- To reach another lane, append to that lane's `## Inbox` in your branch. The
  item reaches that lane when the trunk owner merges your branch and the lane
  next merges `origin/v2`.
- The trunk owner's inbox is the `## Inbox` section of this file.
- Directions in Task 61 and this file override older notes in lane branches,
  including any "Score first, RSS may grow" direction recorded before
  2026-10-10.

## Sync protocol

1. Before starting a change, merge `origin/v2` into the lane branch.
2. After each retained commit, merge `origin/v2` again and push the lane.
3. The trunk owner merges a lane into `v2` only after the lane's change passes
   the M4 gates below. If two lanes built the same mechanism, the trunk keeps
   one implementation and the other lane rebases onto it.
4. Shared facts have one owner: the GC headroom policy lives in the trunk's
   named policy (Task 94 proposes values; the trunk decides), the heap record
   layout lives in Task 61, and the dispatch loop and frame model are shared
   by JavaScript and Wasm.

## Evidence and gates

- Linux results are attribution and screening: Callgrind instruction counts,
  `perf stat` counters and Linux RSS. They choose and reject candidates within
  a lane. Keep or reject on the trunk is decided on the M4.
- M4 gate for any change to `quench-runtime`: the fixed-work instruction and
  RSS screen on the active fixture, clean-cycle pairs, and the resumable
  all-eight guard. The instruction fallback applies only to changes that do
  not alter memory layout. RSS is judged against each fixture's A/A band.
- Correctness for every merged change: `cargo check` with warnings denied, the
  runtime library suite, the affected Test262 stages, the full Wasm directive
  suite when the dispatch loop, frames or Wasm paths change, and the local
  Node oracle for any changed Node behavior.
- Report V8-v7 steps as the active fixture's speed and RSS distance to its
  bars, before and after.
- Commit summaries, hashes and reproduction commands as evidence. Keep
  regenerable raw dumps out of git, or compressed when they must be kept.
