# 079: Generate mechanical dispatch trampolines

`dispatch_handlers.rs` contains hundreds of public handlers; many are small
receiver/argument adapters that only call one module function. Generate these
uniform wrappers from the dispatch declaration and retain handwritten code only
for non-uniform semantics.

## Plan

- Classify wrappers by signature and receiver/argument adaptation.
- Introduce a narrowly typed `host_call!`/`host_construct!` macro or generated
  adapter entries; do not erase meaningful error/effect boundaries.
- Migrate one module family at a time, starting with console/util.
- Delete superseded wrappers and compare identity, descriptors, errors, and
  side effects against Node.

## Acceptance

Mechanical handlers are generated from one declaration, exceptional handlers
remain readable, and the full dispatch/oracle suite is unchanged.
