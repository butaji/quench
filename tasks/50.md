## Scope and implementation sequence

1. Refresh type sizes, allocation counts and retained bytes for the current
   production build. Reuse task 77's selected attribution rows where available;
   do not require an all-domain census.
2. Identify the field or payload responsible for a measured gap. Trace its
   semantic readers, writers, serialization and GC edges before changing its
   representation.
3. Change one representation at a time. Derive layouts and metadata from one
   authority, preserve exact generic behavior, and keep compile-time layout
   assertions only where a specific invariant requires a fixed layout.
4. For strings, keep one semantic unit representation. A cached encoded view
   is derived state; retain it only with explicit lifetime rules and measured
   benefit. Do not prescribe UTF-16, Latin-1 or a rope by analogy.
5. Consider cell variants, object headers, arrays, strings, concatenation and
   suspended frames only when the selected measurements identify that cost.
   Stop with the census when a proposed representation cannot recover a
   meaningful Score/RSS cost.

## Linux compact-cell candidate on 2026-10-08

A current production `-Zprint-type-sizes` census found that `Cell` and `Slot`
were 112 bytes because the `RegExp` payload occupied 112 bytes, with
`ArrayFromAsyncState` at 104 bytes and `Environment` at 101 bytes. The
candidate moves those three cold payloads behind boxes and stores the captured
`with` list as `Box<[Value]>`. `Cell` and `Slot` are 96 bytes (a 14.3% slot
reduction); the `Environment` variant is 93 bytes in the final layout. The
boxed slice retains the same traced values while avoiding a separately boxed
`Vec` header.

On Splay, eleven alternating baseline/candidate pairs lowered median maximum
RSS from 252,465,152 to 235,663,360 bytes (paired median delta
-16,777,216 bytes; 95% interval -16,830,464 to -16,728,064). Median Score
moved from 797 to 808; the paired delta was +25 (95% interval -21 to +39),
which includes a tie. An eleven-pair captured-`with` microbenchmark comparing
the boxed-vector and boxed-slice forms found no supported elapsed-time change
(median +2.51 ms; 95% interval -9.69 to +5.73 ms) and lower maximum RSS
(median -266,240 bytes; interval -573,440 to -45,056). These results support
the memory reduction; they do not establish a Score gain for every fixture.

A Quench-only one-round screen improved Score on seven of eight fixtures,
with RegExp down 0.2. Maximum RSS fell on six fixtures; Crypto and DeltaBlue
rose by 90,112 and 110,592 bytes. This screen is directional and has no
paired confidence interval. The Node v24.19 oracle matched the changed RegExp,
`with`, eval and `Array.fromAsync` scenarios. Test262 passed full stages 8 and
14 (361 cases) plus 95 `Array.fromAsync` and 63 RegExp `lastIndex` cases;
909 affected cases matched across individual, sequential and parallel
`compare-runs` modes.

Keep the compact layout as a pre-gate candidate for its measured RSS benefit.
The Splay Score interval includes a tie, the other fixture screens are single
round, and this experiment did not run the per-engine Stage B qualification or
a new per-kind `profile-memory` census. Raw pairs, type-layout excerpts,
fixture reports, and focused correctness records are in
[`task50-compact-cell-payloads-linux-2026-10-08.json`](evidence/task50-compact-cell-payloads-linux-2026-10-08.json).
