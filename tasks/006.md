termination correctly running through the same resource-destroy path this
task covers — verify whether they need `worker_threads.rs` changes too or
whether they're purely an async_hooks issue once destroy tracking is fixed.

  specific tick relative to GC/task completion — do not guess, check Node's

  catchable/reportable error path, not hang or crash the host)
