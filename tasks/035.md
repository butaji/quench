# 035: Widen region-stencil coverage breadth toward full-catalog parity

- **Theme:** deegen_full_jit
- **Executor:** codex
- **Depends on:** 033

## Context

quench's region-stencil fusion (documented in `docs/copy-and-patch-jit.md`)
is a deliberate, sanctioned extension beyond the paper's one-stencil-per-
bytecode design — that divergence itself is not a gap. But the *breadth* of
coverage is: per the audit, only a handful of hand-enumerated regions exist
today (Number Add+Return, one guarded property region, a two-region
fallthrough — see `RegionKey` constructors in `stencil_select.rs`), far
narrower than the paper's full per-bytecode-variant coverage. This task closes
that breadth gap without abandoning the region-fusion technique.

## Scope
- crates/quench-runtime/src/stencil_select.rs
- crates/quench-runtime/src/stencil_fact.rs
- The build-time stencil catalog generation path

## Deliverables
- Inventory every `vm_op!` catalog entry not yet covered by any admitted
  region or single-opcode stencil
- Extend `RegionKey`/stencil admission to cover the remaining catalog
  entries, reusing the existing build-time proof (single-entry-point CFG
  check, Proven/Guarded fact admission) rather than one-off special cases per
  new region
- Prioritize by neutral-corpus opcode-frequency evidence
  (`docs/architecture-evidence.md`), not by convenience

## Validation

1. Produce the coverage inventory as a concrete artifact: a list/table of
   every `vm_op!` catalog opcode with a column for "covered by a region/
   stencil (Y/N, which RegionKey)". Compute and record the before/after
   coverage ratio (covered opcodes / total catalog opcodes) in
   `docs/deegen-alignment.md`.
2. For each newly admitted region: a build-time proof unit test that its
   single-entry-point CFG check actually rejects a region with an
   externally-reachable interior entry point (construct a synthetic
   counter-example and confirm it's refused admission).
3. Differential tests: identical observable output for programs exercising
   each newly admitted region, and for programs exercising the same opcodes
   in combinations that do NOT match any admitted region (must still hit the
   complete ordinary interpreter correctly).
4. `cargo test -p quench-runtime --lib` — all pass.
5. `node quench-bench/micros/verify.mjs ... --from 1 --to 100 --runs 3` — 100/100, geomean improves, and prioritize regions by the opcode-frequency evidence already in `docs/architecture-evidence.md` (verify the regions you added actually match the highest-frequency uncovered opcodes, not arbitrary ones).

**Fail condition**: an admitted region without its own CFG-soundness test, or
a coverage-ratio claim not backed by the inventory artifact, is incomplete —
this task needs the evidence recorded, not just an assertion that coverage
improved.

## Non-goals
- Changing the fusion technique or lifecycle (021-026, closed) — this is
  coverage breadth only
