# Startup budget for the quench-gate `startup` step (crates/quench-gate/src/startup.rs).
# One row per measurement: `<case> <mode> <metric> <ceiling>`.
#   case: empty (an empty .js), hello (console.log("hi")), module (require of a generated 200-function module).
#   mode: --no-jit, --b-jit, default (no flag, every tier on: the shipped mode).
#   metric: ir (callgrind instructions of the whole run), rss_kb (peak resident set, wait4 ru_maxrss).
# A measurement more than 2% over its ceiling fails. More than 10% under it fails too: lower the ceiling.
# Shrink-only: QUENCH_DEBT_WRITE=1 quench-gate --only startup rewrites the ceilings from the measurements (never up).
