# 10 — Guarded arithmetic and comparison stencils

Status: planned

Add direct int32/Smi-like guarded fast paths first, with checked overflow to the general number path. Add floating-number templates for genuinely floating values. Comparisons and loop updates should stay in the fixed stencil ABI without a generic `Value::number()` helper call on hits.

Templates remain general bytecode implementations generated by Rust macros and rustc/LLVM. No V8v7-specific fusion.

Acceptance: JS coercion/NaN/negative-zero/overflow tests and arithmetic benchmark A/B evidence.
